1. Purpose and Scope
Complaint & Helpdesk Management provides a centralized workflow for receiving, classifying, routing, assigning, monitoring, escalating, resolving and closing resident complaints and service requests. The module also provides SLA monitoring, resident feedback and operational reporting.
Primary objectives
Provide one controlled place to register and track resident complaints/service requests.
Automatically classify and route tickets to the appropriate support team and staff member.
Apply priority and SLA rules consistently.
Give helpdesk teams a practical work queue for daily operations.
Escalate critical, overdue or SLA-risk tickets.
Record resolution, verification, closure and resident feedback.
Provide management with complaint, SLA, escalation, resolution and satisfaction analytics.

4. Detailed Functional Scope
4.1 Helpdesk Dashboard
Show total, new, open, in-progress, unassigned, resolved and closed tickets.
Show SLA on-track, at-risk and breached counts.
Show high/critical priority workload and pending assignments.
Show complaint trends and category/priority summaries.
Show support team/assignee workload and recent activity.
Provide quick actions for Create Ticket, Complaints, Work Queue, SLA Breaches, Escalations and Reports.
4.2 Complaints / Tickets
Search and filter by ticket ID, title, resident, property, unit, category, subcategory, priority, status, team, assignee, SLA status and date.
Display ticket ID, complaint title, resident, unit, category, priority, team, assignee, SLA and current status.
Open Ticket Details.
Edit ticket information where authorized.
Assign/reassign, change priority, add update/comment, add attachment, escalate, resolve, close and reopen where permitted.
4.3 Create Ticket
Capture complaint title and description.
Select resident, contact, property/building/unit and complaint location.
Select category and subcategory.
Set or derive priority.
Upload photos/documents with preview and remove controls.
Capture optional contact/visit information and notes.
Generate unique Ticket ID on submission.
Apply routing, assignment, SLA and notifications after submission.
4.4 My Complaints
Show complaints submitted by the logged-in resident/user.
Search and filter by status, category and date.
Show current status, assigned team, SLA, updates, attachments and resolution.
Allow additional information/attachment updates where permitted.
Allow resident feedback after resolution/closure.
4.5 Work Queue
Provide My Queue, Unassigned, Team Queue, Due Soon, SLA Breached and All Open views.
Show queue counts for open, unassigned, due-soon, breached and high-priority work.
Support search, filters, sorting and refresh.
Allow authorized users to assign, reassign, open, update, escalate and resolve tickets.
4.6 Assignment Management
View unassigned, assigned and reassigned tickets.
Assign a ticket to a support team and staff member.
Reassign ownership and capture reason when required.
Support controlled bulk assignment.
Show assignment history and team workload.
4.7 Categories Management
Create, edit, activate and deactivate complaint categories.
Create and manage subcategories.
Maintain descriptions and default support team.
Display subcategory/routing-rule counts.
Search and filter categories.
Add Category UI should include category name, description, status, subcategories, default team/assignee and routing option.
4.8 Routing Rules
Create, edit, activate/deactivate and manage routing rules.
Define conditions using category, subcategory, priority and supported attributes.
Route to support team and optionally a specific assignee.
Apply an SLA policy through the routing rule.
Define rule order/priority.
Provide Test Routing to simulate a sample complaint.
Maintain rule status and history.
4.9 Priority & SLA Management
Maintain Critical, High, Medium and Low priority levels.
Configure response and resolution targets per priority/policy.
Configure escalation level/trigger.
Configure business hours and 24x7 handling where applicable.
Configure SLA pause conditions such as Awaiting Resident, With Vendor and Pending Approval.
Display On Track, At Risk/Warning, Breached and Completed.
Provide Add/Edit SLA Policy UI and SLA Simulator.
4.10 Escalation Management
Show active, at-risk and breached escalations.
Support manual and rule-driven escalation.
Assign escalation to the appropriate owner.
Support configured escalation levels.
Capture reason, comments, owner and timestamps.
Notify relevant users.
Track escalation progress and history.
4.11 Resolution & Closure
List tickets needing resolution, awaiting verification and ready to close.
Add resolution description and work performed.
Upload resolution evidence.
Record resolved by and resolution date/time.
Submit resolution for verification where configured.
Verify resolution.
Close ticket with closure reason/comments.
Allow reopening under configured business rules.
4.12 Resident Feedback
Trigger feedback request after resolution/closure where configured.
Capture rating and comments.
Track Pending, Submitted and Reviewed states.
Display feedback history and ratings.
Identify low-rated resolutions for management follow-up.
4.13 Complaint / Ticket Details
Provide complete ticket information: ID, title, description, resident, property, unit, category, subcategory and priority.
Show team, assignee and assignment history.
Show response/resolution SLA, remaining time, SLA status and breach details.
Show activity timeline for status changes, comments, assignments, escalations, resolution and closure.
Show photo/document attachments.
Provide authorized actions: Edit, Assign, Reassign, Change Priority, Add Update, Add Attachment, Escalate, Add Resolution, Verify, Close and Reopen.
4.14 Reports & Analytics
Complaint volume by date, property, category, subcategory and status.
SLA compliance, breaches, at-risk tickets, average response time and average resolution time.
Tickets by support team and assignee, unassigned tickets and reassignment counts.
Escalation counts by level/reason and escalation resolution time.
Resolution rate, reopened complaints and average resolution time.
Resident rating and feedback trends.
Support filters for property, building, unit, category, priority, team, assignee, status, SLA status and date range.
Support controlled export to PDF/Excel/CSV where required.
4.15 Helpdesk Settings
Configure ticket numbering and default status.
Configure default priority and category behaviour.
Configure assignment defaults.
Configure SLA defaults, business hours and pause rules.
Configure escalation defaults.
Configure notification preferences.
Configure attachment, reopening and closure rules.

6. End-to-End Business Workflow
Complaint is identified by a resident or authorized staff member.
Ticket is created with resident/location details, category, description and optional photo/document evidence.
System generates a unique Ticket ID.
Category/subcategory and priority are assigned or derived.
Routing rules are evaluated.
Ticket is routed to the configured support team and staff member where applicable.
Applicable SLA policy is attached and response/resolution timers start.
Ticket appears in the appropriate Work Queue.
Assigned staff works on the complaint and records updates, comments and attachments.
SLA is monitored as On Track, At Risk/Warning or Breached.
If escalation conditions are met, the ticket is escalated to the configured level/owner.
Staff records the resolution and supporting evidence.
Resolution is verified where required.
Ticket is closed according to closure rules.
Resident feedback/rating is collected where configured.
Ticket, SLA, escalation, resolution and feedback data become available for reporting.
7. Exception and Alternate Workflows
Unassigned complaint
Ticket created → no valid assignment → Work Queue → manual assignment → continue SLA under configured rules.
SLA at risk
Ticket active → SLA approaching → warning/at-risk → alert → corrective action or escalation.
SLA breach
Target missed → breached → escalation → escalation owner action → resolution.
Reassignment
Assigned → reassign → new team/staff → assignment history updated → notification as configured.
Awaiting Resident / Vendor
Ticket enters waiting state → SLA pauses if configured → response received → SLA resumes → work continues.
Reopened complaint
Closed/resolved → authorized reopen → reason captured → ticket returns to configured working state.
8. Ticket Status Lifecycle
Baseline lifecycle: NEW → OPEN → ASSIGNED → IN PROGRESS → RESOLVED → AWAITING VERIFICATION → CLOSED
Optional working states: Pending, Awaiting Resident, With Vendor, Escalated.
Reopen path: CLOSED → REOPENED → IN PROGRESS.
Status transitions must be controlled by role and configuration.
9. SLA Lifecycle
Ticket Created → SLA Applied → Timer Started → On Track.
As the target approaches, the ticket can become At Risk/Warning.
If the target is missed, the ticket becomes Breached and may trigger escalation.
If the target is completed, the SLA is marked Completed.
Configured waiting states may pause the timer and resume it when the condition ends.
10. Routing Workflow
Ticket Created → Category → Subcategory → Priority → Routing Rule Evaluation → Support Team → Assignee → SLA Policy → Work Queue
Example: Plumbing + Water Leakage + High → Plumbing Team → Rahul Patil → High Priority SLA

16. Business Controls and Validation
Only authorized users can create, assign, escalate, resolve, verify, close, reopen or configure tickets.
Category/subcategory combinations must be valid.
Routing rules must have a deterministic evaluation order.
SLA policy must be applicable to the ticket context.
Ticket ID must be unique.
Required fields must be validated before submission.
Attachment type and size must follow configured limits.
Closure should require resolution information.
Reopening should require authorization and a reason.
Audit history should be protected from normal operational editing.
Dashboard/report figures should use the same lifecycle data as operational screens.
17. Scope Boundaries
Included:
Resident complaint/service-request lifecycle.
Ticket creation, tracking, assignment, routing, priority, SLA, escalation, resolution, closure and feedback.
Operational dashboards, work queues and reports.
Categories, routing, priorities, SLA and helpdesk settings.
Attachments/photos and activity history.
Remain owned by dedicated FMS modules:
Detailed maintenance execution, work orders, technicians and spare consumption.
Resident master data.
Property/unit master data.
Vendor master and contractual administration.
Cross-module accounting/payment processing.
18. Development and UAT Baseline
Use the 15 primary UIs as the main navigation/screen baseline.
Design supporting action UIs for every visible Create/Add/Edit/Assign/Reassign/Escalate/Resolve/Verify/Close/Reopen/Test action.
Implement role-based visibility and action permissions.
Use the ticket and SLA lifecycles as the baseline for backend models and APIs.
Prepare tests for normal, SLA-risk, breach, escalation, reassignment, waiting-state, resolution, closure, reopening and feedback scenarios.
Validate dashboard/report numbers against operational ticket data.
Finalize detailed BRD/SRS, API mapping, data model and UAT cases during implementation.
19. Final Scope Summary
The Complaint & Helpdesk Management module is a complete complaint-to-closure workflow, not simply a ticket list. It contains 15 primary UIs supported by action drawers/modals and embedded tabs/sections.
End-to-end flow: Create → Track → Categorize → Route → Assign → Prioritize → SLA Monitor → Work → Escalate if required → Resolve → Verify → Close → Feedback → Report
All ten Helpdesk feature areas explicitly listed in the uploaded FMS documentation are represented in this scope.
